[SECTION - 03 - THE_APP]

From problem to an actionable tool.

We turned our research into AI Art Detective — a small floating widget for macOS, with Windows on the way. Drop in any image and it returns an AI-generation probability plus the closest matching art style, powered by a cloud-hosted detection model.

01

Detects AI-generated images

Every image you drop in runs through a dedicated AI-vs-human detection model and returns a probability score in seconds.

02

Flags the closest art style

A zero-shot classifier compares the artwork against dozens of styles and movements — from Studio Ghibli to Baroque — to surface what it's likely imitating.

03

Lives on your desktop, not your browser

It's a small always-on-top widget, not a page scanner. Drop in a file from anywhere — no browser or per-site install required.
