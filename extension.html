[SECTION - 03 - THE_EXTENSION]

From problem to an actionable tool.

We turned our research into a browser extension that helps users see where images came from — and whether the people who made them ever agreed to be part of the training set.

01

Detects AI-generated images

Scans images on the page you're viewing and flags likely AI-generated content using model-fingerprint detection.

02

Surfaces the source

Where possible, links an image back to its likely original creator or training-set origin, restoring attribution the model stripped away.

03

Shows the consent status

Marks whether an image's creator has opted out of AI training datasets, so you can make an informed choice before sharing.

[HOW_IT_HELPS]

It gives the choice back to the viewer.

The extension doesn't block AI art or judge it. It makes the hidden context visible — who made a thing, whether they consented, and whether what you're looking at was written by a person or sampled by a model.
