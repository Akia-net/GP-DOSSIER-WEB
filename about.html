[WHY_THIS_TOPIC]

Generative Artificial Intelligence (AI involves training algorithms to generate unique content such as images, music, and texts as well as in the form of arts. However, this technological shift introduces new legal and ethical questions regarding authorship and intellectual property rights. Human artists worldwide face licensing conflicts from AI training models, which replace human art appreciation. Unfortunately, current copyright laws have not demonstrated full protection against generative AI. Since offices and courts proposed the “Human-Only Rule”, AI-generated works lack human authorship and cannot get copyright. Therefore, this study explores the core research question: “To what extent does current copyright law protect human artists’ rights against generative AI?”.
